The Plainsguitar reference

Tuning D A D E A D (low→high) Capo 4 Key F# 75 BPM Frets relative to capo (0 = capo)
Reference tracks: Champ by Gleemer, Acheron by DIIV

Arrangement

Full song order. The interlude is the intro pattern again; the outro just rides the final B of the last chorus and then we ring out.

Intro→ V1→ PC1→ C1→ Interlude→ V2→ PC2→ C2→ Bridge→ C3→ Outro (B)

Effects

Verse / Intro / InterludeClean, healthy dose of CE-2W-style chorus. A flanger dialed back into a subtle chorus (Cure-style) would work fine too.
Intro / interlude leadMulti-head tape delay in a kind of cascading dotted-8th pattern. Can try out some shades of reverb as well.
Chorus rhythmHeavy fuzz / distortion for a big pummeling wall.
Bridge & last-chorus leadsSame delay + modulation as the intro lead, bit of overdrive on top for grit.

Progression
