Violetsguitar reference

Tuning D A D E A D (low→high) Capo 4 Key Db 138 BPM Frets relative to capo (0 = capo)
Reference track: Prism by Seventeen Years Old and Berlin Wall

Arrangement

The verses end with a short two-chord tag that works like a loose pre-chorus rather than a full section; the interlude is the intro again.

Intro→ V1→ C1→ Interlude→ V2→ C2→ Bridge→ C3

Effects

Big sections · rhythmIntro, interlude, chorus and bridge are big fuzz / distortion walls, but the rhythm shouldn't need much reverb at all and should retain some sparkle.
Big sections · leadReverb into a fuzz for the continuous hazy wash, want a lot of sustain so the lead lines are kind of wailing.
Verses · rhythmBacks off to clean with a bit of breakup.
Verses · leadAlso backs off, but some overdrive is still called for.
Clean tonesChorus and/or flanger on the clean parts.

▸ Verse / Pre-chorus

Arpeggio

Rolls up and back down. ×4 with the bass on the open 5th string, then ×4 with it on the open 6th string. That whole 8-bar block repeats twice. Over the pre-chorus it becomes a rolling 5th–3rd–2nd–1st–2nd–3rd cell that cycles, the lower notes drop down to the 5th fret as it goes.

▸ Bridge

High wash

A high tremolo line over the bridge. Same wash approach as the chorus (reverb into fuzz, pick count loose). It descends on the 2nd string (7 5 4 2 4 2 0), alternates the open 2nd string with 1st string at 2, then lands on 2 + 2 across the top two strings. All ×2. Then 2 → 7 → 6 on the top string, ×3.

High picked line

One more high picked line over the last chorus, more rhythmic. First phrase descends: 1st@7, then 7 7 7 4 4 4 5 on the 2nd string, then 5 5 5 on the 3rd, played ×2.
